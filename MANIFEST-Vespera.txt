VESPERA 长庚 / Display and Mono
Vespera Display 0.0.1 (OpenType 0.001) / proportional spacing / Light, Regular, Bold; roman and italic
Vespera Mono 0.0.1 (OpenType 0.001) / monospaced / Light, Regular, Bold; roman and italic
Modified work based on TeX Gyre Pagella Regular and Italic 2.501 (kept as
plain-text sources); advances, ink placement and kerning of Vespera Display
follow TeX Gyre Pagella X, Pagella X Italic, Bold and BoldItalic (newpx),
stored as extracted parameters.
Maintenance status of this modified work: unmaintained.
Date of modification: 2026-11-17.

Both families are generated from one drawing master in fontviber/ and share
its letters, wedge serifs, lozenge dots, source punctuation and stroke model.
Display takes Pagella X's advances, ink positions and kerning, and redraws
the letters only: carved 45-degree cuts on every stroke end, restrained
wedge serifs (5% reach), a Wei Bei movement
(horizontals rising about 4.3 degrees, mid-height lifted 4.5%) and a light
optical treatment (+6 vertical units). Mono refits the same
drawings to a 640-unit cell (serif retraction,
stem-compensated condensing, serif extension for narrow letters) and adds all 128
box-drawing characters, block elements and Powerline separators.
Each family is also packed as one OpenType Collection (.otc) holding all six
styles; every member is byte-identical, table for table, to its standalone OTF.

Math operators are reduced to 80% about the math axis with stroke compensation.
Light and Bold apply (-26, -6) and (+50, +18) unit stroke gains; Bold is then
brought back inward to Pagella X Bold's widths. The roman uses
single-storey a and g. Italics are the TeX Gyre Pagella Italic cursive with
longer entry and exit strokes and swash feet on A, M and N, at 10 degrees.
OpenType layout tables (GSUB, GPOS) come from the source; mark anchors are
transformed with each glyph's outline. Mono keeps only ccmp, locl (Romanian
and Moldavian comma-below s and t), mark and mkmk: no ligatures or kerning.

The original source font and notices are retained. The complete original
TeX Gyre work is available at https://ctan.org/tex-archive/fonts/tex-gyre .
Font distribution follows licenses/GUST-FONT-LICENSE.txt and licenses/lppl.txt.

中文说明
字体中文名「长庚」：Vespera 是拉丁语的「黄昏」，与 Vesper（长庚星）同源。
Vespera Display 0.0.1（OpenType 0.001）：比例字距；细体、常规、粗体，各含正体与斜体。
Vespera Mono 0.0.1（OpenType 0.001）：等宽；细体、常规、粗体，各含正体与斜体。
本作品是 TeX Gyre Pagella Regular 与 Italic 2.501 的修改版本（源字体以纯文本
保存）；Vespera Display 的字宽、墨迹位置与字偶距沿用 newpx 的 TeX Gyre Pagella X、
Pagella X Italic、Bold 与 BoldItalic，以提取出的参数保存。
本修改作品的维护状态：不再维护。修改日期：2026-11-17。

两个字族由 fontviber/ 中的同一份设计母版生成，共用字母、楔形衬线、菱形点、源字体
标点与笔画模型。Display 沿用 Pagella X 的字宽、墨迹位置与字偶距，只重绘字形：所有
笔画末端约 45° 刀刻斜切、克制的楔形衬线（外延 5%）、魏碑笔势（横画右上扬约 4.3°、
字腰上提 4.5%），以及轻度光学处理（竖笔 +6 单位）。Mono 把同一套字形适配到 640
单位字宽（收短衬线、按竖笔补偿压缩、为窄字母延长衬线），并加入全部 128 个制表符、块元素与
Powerline 分隔符。每个字族另打包为一个包含全部 6 个样式的 OpenType 合集（.otc），
其中每个成员与对应的单独 OTF 逐表、逐字节相同。

数学运算符以数学轴为中心缩小到 80%，并补偿笔画粗细。Light 与 Bold 的笔画增量分别为
(-26, -6) 与 (+50, +18) 单位，Bold 随后向内收回到 Pagella X Bold 的字宽。正体使用
单层 a 与 g。斜体为 TeX Gyre Pagella Italic 的书写体，入笔与出笔加长，A、M、N 带
花式脚，倾斜 10°。OpenType 布局表（GSUB、GPOS）取自源字体，变音符锚点随每个字形
的轮廓一同变换。Mono 只保留 ccmp、locl（罗马尼亚语与摩尔多瓦语的下加逗号 s、t）、
mark 与 mkmk，不含连字与字偶距。

原始源字体及其声明均予保留。完整的 TeX Gyre 原作见
https://ctan.org/tex-archive/fonts/tex-gyre 。字体按 licenses/GUST-FONT-LICENSE.txt
与 licenses/lppl.txt 分发。下面的文件清单与 SHA-256 适用于两种语言。
